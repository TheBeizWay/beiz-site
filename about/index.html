About Beiz

Beiz Data & Accounting Pty Ltd builds and tests finance workflows across the systems Australian businesses already use, with the controls a Chartered Accountant expects. Outsourced bookkeeping and finance firms and in-house finance teams first.

Chartered Accountant, a member of Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ) with a Certificate of Public Practice and a graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors, with a background in audit, controls, finance, data analytics and building AI automation.
