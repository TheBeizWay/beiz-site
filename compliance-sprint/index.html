Common questions

How long does the compliance controls sprint take?

Two weeks, on one focus: AI use, AML/CTF Tranche 2 or the Privacy Act. A second focus is quoted in writing.

What does it cost?

From $3,500, a fixed price agreed in writing before we start. Beiz isn't registered for GST, so no GST is added.

Do you need access to our ledger or systems?

No. The sprint runs on short interviews and the documents you already have.

What do we get at the end?

A register of what's in scope, a data map, a named owner for each obligation, the rules and approval points, and a 90-day plan for the first workflow worth running under controls.

Do you write our AML/CTF program or privacy policy?

No. We don't give legal advice. Program and policy wording stays with your adviser or lawyer, and we hand them the facts.

Which obligation worries you most?

Tell us in a few lines. You'll get a written reply on where to start.
