Is your firm caught?

It depends on the services you provide, not on being an accountant. The regulated services accounting firms most often provide are:

  • Setting up or restructuring companies, trusts and partnerships
  • Acting as a director, company secretary, trustee or partner for a client, or arranging for someone else to
  • Acting as a nominee shareholder, or arranging one
  • Providing a registered office or principal place of business address
  • Helping a client buy, sell or transfer a business, or interests in a company or trust
  • Helping with equity or debt financing
  • Receiving, holding or controlling client money or property as part of those services
  • Helping a client buy, sell or transfer real estate

A practice that only does tax returns, BAS and bookkeeping is probably outside scope. If you provide any of the services above, even occasionally, check them against AUSTRAC's guidance for tranche 2 entities or with your adviser.

Key dates

  • 1 July 2026: obligations started
  • Within 28 days of first providing a regulated service: enrol with AUSTRAC
  • Every three years: independent evaluation of your program

What your firm has to do

  • Enrol with AUSTRACWithin 28 days of first providing a regulated service.
  • An AML/CTF programA written risk assessment and policies, approved by a senior manager, kept up to date and independently evaluated at least every three years.
  • A compliance officerA fit and proper person who runs the program day to day. In a sole practice, that can be the principal.
  • Customer due diligenceIdentify and verify the client, and the people who own or control it, before you provide the service. Then keep monitoring.
  • ReportsA suspicious matter report within three business days (24 hours for terrorism financing), and a threshold transaction report for physical cash of $10,000 or more.
  • Records and an annual reportKeep records for at least seven years, and report each year on how you met your obligations.

Questions firms ask

We only do tax returns, BAS and bookkeeping. Are we caught?

Probably not, on those services alone. It turns on what you do, not on being an accountant. If you provide any of the services listed above, even occasionally, check them with AUSTRAC's guidance or your adviser.

Can an AI agent be our AML/CTF compliance officer?

No. The compliance officer has to be a fit and proper person. The agent prepares the checks; your officer makes the decisions and signs off.

What about clients we had before 1 July 2026?

AUSTRAC's summary says you generally only need to do due diligence on existing clients when a suspicious matter report is due or their risk changes significantly to medium or high.

Do you need access to our ledger or client files?

Not for the sprint. It runs on short interviews and the documents you already have. If you go on to set up an agent, it works inside your own systems and screening service, with access agreed in writing.
