How fees work

Every engagement is a fixed fee, agreed in writing before any work starts. You see the fee, the timeline and what done looks like before you commit.

How a fee is set

Scope first, then a fixed fee in writing

Talk or write

A 20-minute call or a few lines in writing about the work. You'll get a written reply by the next business day.

Scope

We agree what's in and out, which records we need and who signs off at each step. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.

Proposal

Within three business days: a fixed fee, a timeline and a definition of done, in writing. Nothing starts until you accept it.

No surprises

The fee doesn't move unless the scope does, and any change is agreed in writing first. Beiz isn't registered for GST, so no GST is added.

Terms

Terms that matter

Credit

The fee for the two-week cash and data check is credited once against your next Beiz build (cash model, AI workforce setup, compliance sprint or leave cover setup) if you accept the proposal and pay the deposit within 60 days of the findings. It isn't redeemable for cash and doesn't apply to monthly support.

Monthly operations

Covers up to two agreed agents or one model: a monthly control review, a log sample, a test re-run and up to two hours of fixes in total. More is quoted. Requests are acknowledged within two business days. It isn't round-the-clock monitoring or an emergency service.

Cash model

You drop in fresh exports weekly and the model refreshes. Beiz builds it, ties actuals to the ledger, documents the assumptions and hands it over. Beiz reviews the forecast only where the support agreement sets out how often and what.

Proposals

Every proposal has a fixed fee, a timeline and a definition of done, and arrives within three business days.
