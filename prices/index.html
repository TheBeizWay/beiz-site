How a quote works

  1. A 20-minute fit call, early morning or after 5pm.
  2. A written proposal within three business days: what we'll do, the fixed price, the timeline, what we need from you and what's excluded.
  3. A definition of done. Every proposal lists the things that will exist at the end and how we'll both check them, so the job has a finish line.
  4. Changes in writing. Anything outside the scope is quoted before any work on it starts.

The $1,500 cash & data check is credited against the next piece of work if you go ahead.

Never in scope

  • Tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements
  • Statutory audit or assurance
  • Payments released without a person
  • Legal advice or program wording

When you don't need us

Use what you already have

  • The work happens in one system, on a simple rule: the bank rules and bill approvals built into your accounting software.
  • One person needs help drafting, summarising or searching: the AI licence your team already pays for.
  • It happens a few times a month and a checklist would do.

An agent is worth setting up

  • The work crosses systems: ledger, job or practice software, inbox and screening service.
  • Records have to be checked against each other before anyone acts.
  • Your limits and approvers have to be enforced every time, with a log someone can review later.

If your own software can do it, we'll say so on the fit call and you won't pay for a build.

Not sure where to start?

You'll talk to Purav, not a sales team. Bring the hard questions. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.
