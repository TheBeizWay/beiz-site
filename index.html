New · Leave cover

Someone's going on leave. The work doesn't have to stop.

Parental leave, long service leave, a month in Europe. Bills still arrive, invoices still need to go out and someone has to watch the cash. We set up an agent to carry the routine part of their role, with an Australian Chartered Accountant reviewing its work, and hand everything back the day they return.

  1. Before they goWe shadow them for a week and write the agent's job description from how they actually do the work. They sign it off.
  2. While they're awayThe agent matches bills, drafts invoices and reminders and keeps the reconciliation current. Anything unusual is held for a named person. You get a one-page update every Friday.
  3. When they're backA handover note: what was done, what was held and what's waiting. The agent can stay on to help them, or be switched off.

Their job stays theirs. The agent covers routine work only. Decisions, customers and anything about people stay with your team. Best booked three to four weeks before the leave starts.
