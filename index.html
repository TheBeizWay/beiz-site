One firm, four disciplines

AI agencies can build but can't sign off the numbers. Accountants can sign off but rarely build. Beiz does both, under one name.

Data

Your systems joined, cleaned and reconciled, so every report starts from the same numbers.

AI

Agents that do the repetitive work, with clear limits on what each one can touch.

Accounting

Chartered Accountant judgement on cash, margins, models and controls.

Governance

Board-level thinking (GAICD) on risk, approvals and AI use policy.

Every agent gets a job description, like any new hire

That's the difference between an AI workforce and a pile of chatbots. Each agent has a role, the tools it may use, a delegation limit and someone it reports to. We write those the way a Chartered Accountant and company director would for staff.

Co-pilot

The agent drafts and a person approves. We use it for payments, customer emails and anything about a person.

Autopilot, within limits

The agent acts alone on low-risk, rule-based work like matching, reconciling and reminders you've approved. It stops at the limit and asks.

What we don't do

  • Tax returns, BAS or audit. Your accountant keeps that work. We work alongside them and share what we find.
  • Lock you in. Everything is built in your accounts and handed over.
  • Let AI decide on its own. Anything that moves money, reaches a customer or affects a person needs a human yes.
