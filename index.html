    How every agent is set up

    Written down, locked down, tested, watched.

    1. Declare

      One job, written down and signed off by you: what the agent does, what it never does, the limit it stops at and the person it reports to.

    2. Contain

      It can only reach what that job needs. Logins stay in your own password manager, and no agent has permission to pay anyone or send anything outside your business.

    3. Test

      Before it touches live work, it runs over a few months of your past records and each result is checked against what actually happened. It goes live only at a pass mark agreed with you in writing.

    4. Watch

      Every action is logged in your own accounts. We review the log monthly and re-test before any change.
