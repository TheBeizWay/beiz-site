AI without controls is a risk. AI with controls is a workforce.

AI is arriving inside the software you already use, and on its own it only sees that one system. We set the limits, approvers and decision log for the AI you already have, and build the agents that connect your systems, so every action is:

  • Based on your own recordsFigures trace back to your ledger, timesheets and quotes.
  • Inside limits you setEach agent has a job description and a delegation limit.
  • Logged where you can see itWho decided what, and why, in a log you own.

You ask. The agents act, within the limits you set.

  • It makes up a number. A forecast built on a figure that isn't in your books. Beiz: figures are traced to the ledger, and anything that can't be is held.
  • It promises a customer something you don't offer. In Moffatt v Air Canada (2024 BCCRT 149) a tribunal held an airline liable for a refund policy its chatbot made up. Beiz: anything going to a customer stays a draft until a person sends it.
  • It leaks your data. Staff paste client files into free AI tools. Beiz: only AI plans whose terms exclude training on your data, and least access.
  • It drifts and nobody notices. Supplier formats, prices and AI models change. Beiz: we review the decision log every month and re-test before any change goes live.

Why an Australian Chartered Accountant in the loop

Your agents will work under Australian rules: the Privacy Act, GST and BAS, super and payroll. An Australian Chartered Accountant knows those rules and answers to the APES 110 Code of Ethics and a professional standards scheme approved under Australian law.

Most AI projects put someone in the loop who understands only part of the problem. At Beiz the person who designs and reviews the loop is Purav Mehta, and he covers all four parts:

Three questions to ask anyone setting up your AI

  • Who is responsible for how the agents behave?A named Chartered Accountant designs each agent's limits and reviews its work. Your people approve the decisions that matter.
  • What can each agent touch, and who set that limit?A written job description and delegation limit for every agent, agreed with you before it goes live.
  • What happens if the builder moves on?You own every agent, with documentation, limits and logs any competent firm can pick up.
