    Straight answers
    Questions we get asked.
    

    

    Why pay Beiz when anyone can use ChatGPT?
    A chatbot gives you an answer. Beiz gives you a system that runs on your own ledger, timesheets and bank data, is tested against your past records, and is led by a Chartered Accountant who holds professional indemnity insurance. When a board, bank or regulator asks who is accountable for the work, there is a name on it.
    

    What changes on 10 December 2026 under the Privacy Act?
    Organisations covered by the Privacy Act must say in their privacy policy when a computer program uses personal information to make, or substantially help make, decisions that could significantly affect people. Most finance teams have several: credit holds, approvals and onboarding checks. Our compliance sprint maps them and names an owner for each.
    

    Do you replace our accountant or tax agent?
    No. Beiz does not do tax returns, BAS, statutory audit or legal advice. We build the reporting, systems and controls around your existing accountant, and work alongside accounting firms rather than competing with them.
    

    We already pay for workflow software. Why would we need you?
    Often you don't, and the fit conversation is where we find out. Where we help is the part between the tools: configuring the workflow, removing the manual handoffs, testing it on your own files and getting the team using it.
    

    How do you know it worked?
    Before we build, we agree in writing the inputs, the rules, what a complete output looks like and who reviews exceptions. After handover we compare manual handling time, corrections, open exceptions and time to finish with where you started, including review time.
    

    How fast can you start?
    Fit conversations within a week. Diagnostics start within two weeks of sign-off, and the sprint is scheduled from the diagnostic scope.
    

    Who owns what you build?
    You do. Dashboards, scripts, prompts and decision logs live in your own accounts with documentation. No licence fee, no lock-in.
