How the check works

Two weeks, three steps, one fixed price.

  1. You send exports

    Bills, invoices and timesheets from Xero and your job software, up to 12 months. Exports only: no logins, nothing installed.

  2. We check them against each other

    Bills against timesheets and quotes, hours worked against invoices, invoices against your rate card, credits and overdue accounts.

  3. You get the answers

    A findings register with the record behind every item, a two-page report and a 30-minute walkthrough. $1,500, credited if you go ahead with anything else.
