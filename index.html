See an AI workflow, one step at a time.

An enquiry becomes a checked, drafted reply and an onboarding checklist. AI reads, rules check, and a person decides. Use the steps or your arrow keys.

Interactive simulation · synthetic data · nothing is sent
Step 1 · Input

An enquiry arrives with a brief attached.

Real enquiries are messy: several requests in one email, details missing, a typo in a number. This one is made up.

From: Mia Tran <mia@harbourline.example>Subject: Help with supplier bills
Hi, We're a 9-person joinery business. Our two office staff spend most of Monday keying supplier bills from email into Xero, about 120 a month. We'd like any bill over $5,000 to need my approval before it's paid. Could you also help with our BAS? We'd like something running before Christmas. Brief attached. Mia Tran Operations Manager, Harbourline Joinery Pty Ltd ABN 12 345 678 901
harbourline-brief.pdf · syntheticSystems: Xero, Outlook and a job app (we're changing provider in January). Bills arrive as PDFs and phone photos, and some suppliers email the same bill twice. Goal: Monday mornings back.

Step 3 · Rules

Fixed rules check it, and flag what's missing.

Rules do the checks that must give the same answer every time. The ABN check below actually runs in your browser using the standard ABN formula.

RuleResultStatus
Required details presentContact, workflow and volume found. Budget and decision-maker missing.held
ABN format and checksum12 345 678 901: fails the standard ABN check. Don't create a client record until confirmed.fail
Scope boundaryMentions BAS. Tax and BAS agent services are outside Beiz's scope; the reply must say so.flag
Data in the attachmentNo bank account, card or tax file number patterns found in the brief.pass
Conflict checkNot automated. A person runs it before any proposal.person

Missing or uncertain

  • Budget not statedAsk for a range. Don't guess one.
  • Who approves the project?The AI inferred Mia, with low confidence. An operations manager may not sign contracts.
  • The job app is changing in JanuaryThe AI suggested a product name with low confidence. Guesses aren't passed on. The build should not depend on the old app.
  • ABN doesn't validateProbably a typo. Ask, rather than correcting it silently.
  • BAS is out of scopeSay so plainly and point to their tax agent.
Step 4 · AI drafts

A suggested next step, a draft reply and an onboarding checklist.

Suggested next step: offer a 20-minute call. Likely fit: workflow automation for supplier bills, fixed scope. The draft asks for the gaps instead of guessing them.

Step 5 · Person

Nothing moves until a person decides.

Here a person reads the draft and the flags, then approves, edits or rejects it. The reason is logged either way.
