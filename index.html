    Governance

    One problem, four lenses: "Our job reports never agree." Data traces where each number comes from and fixes the records. Accounting defines what margin means and reconciles it to the books. AI takes over the repetitive checks and drafts the monthly commentary. Governance decides who owns each definition and who signs off a change.
