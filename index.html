How it stays safe

Nothing runs on its own on day one

  1. 01

    Declare

    Each agent gets a written job description, its limits and a named person it reports to.

  2. 02

    Contain

    It works only in the systems you approve, with a monthly spending cap and an off switch. Nothing is paid or sent without a person.

  3. 03

    Test

    It's tested on your past records against a pass mark agreed in writing, then runs alongside your staff in shadow mode.

  4. 04

    Watch

    Every decision is logged in your own accounts, and we review it with you at day 30.

Beiz doesn't do tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements, tax or BAS agent services, statutory audit or assurance, legal advice or financial product advice, and never releases payments without a person. Your tax agent keeps that work. How a build runs.
