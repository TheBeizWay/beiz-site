Automation where it helps. Human approval where it matters.

Here's what that looks like in practice. This is our own build, tested on sample data. It isn't a client result.

The problem

Supplier bills arrive by email. Someone keys them in, checks them by eye and pays. Duplicates, changed bank details and price jumps slip through.

What we built

A workflow that reads each bill, matches it to the supplier and the last price paid, and prepares the payment run in the accounting software.

The controls

Anything over the approval limit, a likely duplicate, new bank details or a price jump is held for a person, with the reason written down. Nothing is paid automatically.

The test

Eight sample bills: four ready to pay ($5,468) and four held for a person ($26,375), each for the right reason.
