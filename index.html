You've done the work. Has it all been invoiced?

In a project firm, money slips out between the timesheet, the job and the Xero invoice. Hours that never get billed. Fixed fees that overrun with no variation raised. Jobs closed before the last invoice goes out. We find it, put a dollar figure on it, and fix the process so it stops.

Six places project firms lose money they've already earned

Each one is small on its own. Together they're worth knowing about, and none of them shows up in your profit and loss until it's too late to bill.

  • Utilisation looks fine. Why is revenue flat?Unbilled hoursTime is logged against a job but never makes it onto an invoice.
  • We won the job. Why did we lose money on it?Fixed fees that overrunScope grows, the team keeps working, and nobody raises a variation.
  • That job's finished. Why haven't we been paid?The last invoiceJobs are closed while the final milestone sits in draft or is never raised.
  • Who agreed to that rate?Rates that driftOld rate cards, discounts nobody approved, the wrong role on a timesheet.
  • How much do we give away?Quiet write-offsClient work marked non-billable as goodwill, without anyone deciding to.
  • Why does month-end take a week?Copying between systemsSomeone moves numbers from the job system into Xero by hand, and things fall through.

The revenue leakage diagnostic

A fixed-scope piece of work with one output: what's leaking, how much, and what to change first.

  1. A 20-minute call

    You tell us how billing works today. We tell you straight whether a diagnostic is worth doing. If it isn't, we'll say so.

  2. Read-only exports

    Your Xero invoices and a timesheet export. No logins to your systems and no changes to them.

  3. About two weeks

    Every hour and every invoice matched to a job. The gaps checked with your project managers, so we know which are mistakes and which were your call.

  4. A one-hour readout

    One page: a dollar figure, the jobs to look at first, and three fixes in order of effort.

Fixed fee, quoted in writing before we start. If you go ahead with the fixes, the diagnostic fee comes off the price.

Software can find the gaps. Someone still has to stand behind the number.

  • A Chartered Accountant signs the findings. Professional indemnity cover, and liability limited under a professional standards scheme.
  • We ask why. A spreadsheet shows the gap. A five-minute conversation with your project manager explains it.
  • We separate leakage from fair write-offs. Not every non-billable hour is a mistake, and we won't call it one.
  • You deal with the person doing the work, from the first call to the handover.
  • It isn't an audit. It's a commercial review for you to act on, with no sign-off from us on your accounts.
