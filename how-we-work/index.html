How it's built

Rules first. AI only for the exceptions.

Most finance work follows rules, so rules do most of the work. That keeps every step traceable and the running costs low.

Rules first

Fixed rules do the routine checking: matching amounts, dates, suppliers and invoice numbers. They behave the same way every time and cost nothing to run.

AI for the exceptions

AI only looks at what the rules can't settle, like a supplier invoice laid out in an unusual way. It hands back a structured answer for the rules to check again.

A person decides

Anything held, unusual or over your limit goes to a named person. Every run is kept in a history your staff can read, step by step.

Every agent

What every Beiz build includes

  • A written job description, limits and a named person the agent reports to
  • Testing on your past records against a pass mark agreed in writing
  • Shadow mode before live
  • A decision log kept in your own accounts
  • A monthly spending cap and an off switch
  • Nothing paid or sent without a person
  • A day-30 review
  • An operating folder you keep: business context, procedures and agent memory, as plain files

What we don't do

Beiz doesn't do tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements, tax or BAS agent services, statutory audit or assurance, legal advice or financial product advice, and never releases payments without a person. Your tax agent keeps that work.
