How we work

How we work.

From first message to handover, and what stays with you and your other advisers.

Seven parts of a working system

  1. Understand the financial problem

    What it costs, who it affects and what good looks like in the numbers.

  2. Redesign the workflow

    Take out handoffs and re-keying before automating anything.

  3. Connect the data

    Bring the systems together so every step uses the same facts.

  4. Build and test

    Tested on your past records against a pass mark agreed in writing.

  5. Handle exceptions

    Decide what happens when something is missing, odd or unclear.

  6. Establish controls

    Approval points, limits and a log of every decision.

  7. Support adoption

    Training, documentation and a review once it's running.
