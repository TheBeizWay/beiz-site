Data engineering

One version of the numbers.

Your data lives in Xero, a CRM, a job app, a POS and a few spreadsheets. We connect it, clean it and keep it flowing, so your dashboards, models and AI all work from the same trusted information.

The problem

Every report starts with someone copying and pasting. Revenue means three different things in three systems. Nobody trusts the numbers enough to act on them, and any AI you add just repeats the mess faster.

What we do

  1. Map it. Every system, what it holds, who owns it, and where the numbers disagree.
  2. Define it. A plain-English data dictionary: what each number means and where it comes from.
  3. Connect it. Automated pipelines that pull data from your tools on a schedule, with checks that flag missing or odd records.
  4. Serve it. One clean source that your dashboards, models and AI assistants all use.

Right-sized tools

We use what fits your size: n8n or Power Automate for the flows, Python for the heavy lifting, and a simple database or your existing SharePoint or Google Drive where that's enough. No platform you don't need.

What gets in the way (we'll tell you upfront)

  • Messy source data. Unreconciled accounts, duplicate customers and bills that never get entered. A pipeline moves data faster; it doesn't make it right. Clean-up comes first, and we'll say if it's a bigger job than the build.
  • Systems that don't talk. Some software has no proper connection, or limits how much you can pull. Then the honest answer is a scheduled export, not a fragile workaround.
  • Things change. Vendors update their systems and pipelines break. Every build has alerts when a run fails and a runbook for fixing it, and keeping it running is a small, ongoing job, not set-and-forget.
  • Where data lives. Some cloud tools store data overseas. If your data needs to stay in Australia, we host it here, and that's decided before we build, not after.
  • AI isn't a calculator. AI can read, sort and summarise. The numbers themselves come from your systems and are checked by a person, never made up by a model.

What you get

  • Data mapEvery system and how they connect.
  • Data dictionaryWhat each number means, its source and its owner.
  • Automated pipelinesScheduled, with checks and alerts.
  • Clean data storeOne source for reports, models and AI.
  • Quality checksMissing, duplicate and odd records flagged.
  • RunbookWhat runs when, and what to do if it stops.
