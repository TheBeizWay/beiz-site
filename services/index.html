Who it's for, and the limits we work to

Who it's for

Outsourced bookkeeping and finance firms of around 5 to 25 staff, and Australian businesses and finance teams with one month-end job that keeps getting stuck.

Hard limits

Works only in the systems you approve. Tested on your past records against a pass mark agreed in writing. Shadow mode before anything goes live. Nothing is paid or sent without a person. Every decision logged in your own accounts.

Who reviews

An Australian Chartered Accountant (Certificate of Public Practice, GAICD) scopes the work and reviews every deliverable before handover. An agent never signs anything off.

What we won't do

Tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements, tax or BAS agent services, statutory audit or assurance, legal advice, financial product advice, or payments released without a person.
