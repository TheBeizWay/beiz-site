What we do

Use AI in your business, with guardrails.

AI agents do the routine checking, drafting and preparation. An Australian Chartered Accountant sets their limits and reviews every exception. Every engagement is fixed price, agreed in writing, and ends with something your team can run.

Fixed prices, agreed in writing

Four services.

  1. $1,500 fixed · credited if you go ahead

    Find the money you're losing

    Two-week cash & data check. From your Xero and job or timesheet exports we find unbilled work, margin leaks and manual bottlenecks, with the records behind each finding.

  2. From $3,500 · two weeks

    Get your compliance controls in place

    Compliance controls sprint. One focus: AI use, AML/CTF Tranche 2 or the Privacy Act. You get a register, named owners, the rules and approval points, and a 90-day plan.

  3. From $5,000

    See your cash 13 weeks ahead

    Live cash & job profit model. A 13-week rolling cash flow and profit by job against what you quoted, updated weekly, in your own Excel or Power BI.

  4. From $8,500 · three-week rollout

    Put AI agents to work in your accounts

    AI workforce setup. Your first agent checks bills, reconciles or prepares client checks inside your own accounts, tested on your past records, with a person approving what matters.

Not sure yet? Start with the free bill check. After setup, monthly controls operations from $1,250 a month and finance leave cover from $3,500. Prices are confirmed in writing before work starts. Beiz isn't registered for GST, so no GST is added.
