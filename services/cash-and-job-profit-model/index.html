Services

Cash & job profit model

A 13-week cash view and profit by job or client, tied back to the ledger.

In brief
  • For businesses that need to see the tight cash week before it arrives, not after month-end.
  • A 13-week cash view built from when your customers actually pay, your payroll, super and tax dates, and profit by job, tied to the ledger.
  • A model with written assumptions that you run after handover. Not financial product advice.
The problem

The bank balance and last month's profit and loss don't show which week cash gets tight or which jobs make money.

What you get

What you get

  • A 13-week cash forecast that refreshes from your weekly exports
  • Profit by job or client, reconciled to the ledger
  • Written assumptions and a handover session

Limits

  • A model and its assumptions, not financial product advice
  • You run it after handover; ongoing review only where agreed in writing
