Automated-Decision Privacy Sprint.

A fixed-price sprint for organisations covered by the Privacy Act, including health and allied health providers and businesses over $3 million. We find what your software decides about people, and hand your lawyer what they need to update your privacy policy before 10 December 2026.

Why now

From 10 December 2026, if the Privacy Act covers you, your privacy policy has to say when computer programs make, or substantially help make, decisions that could significantly affect people, and what personal information they use. Most businesses don't yet know which of their tools do that. It's often buried inside booking, finance, HR and fraud software.

Who it's for

  • Health and allied health providers, who are generally covered regardless of turnover
  • Businesses with annual turnover over $3 million
  • Businesses that trade in personal information

Not sure you're covered? Take the free 2-minute check first.

How the sprint runs

  1. Inventory: every system and AI tool that touches customer, patient, staff or applicant information, the free ones included.
  2. Decision map: for each, what it decides or shapes, what personal information it uses, and how significant the outcome is for the person.
  3. Human review: where a person genuinely reviews outcomes, and where they should.
  4. Handover to your lawyer: plain-English draft wording describing your automated decisions, with the evidence behind it, ready for legal review.

We don't give legal advice. The sprint produces the facts and draft wording; your lawyer confirms the final privacy policy.

What you get

  • System & AI tool inventoryIncluding tools staff adopted on their own.
  • Automated-decision mapWhat decides what, using which personal information.
  • Significance assessmentWhich decisions could significantly affect people.
  • Human-review designWhere a person must genuinely check outcomes.
  • Draft policy wordingPlain English, for your lawyer to finalise.
  • One-page summaryFor the board, the owner or the practice manager.
