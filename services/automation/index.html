Workflow automation

One repetitive finance or admin workflow automated, tested and handed over.

In brief
  • For teams doing the same matching, chasing and checking every week.
  • One workflow automated with fixed rules for the routine records and AI only for the exceptions, then tested and handed over.
  • Runs in your own accounts, with a run history your staff can read and a person approving anything that matters.
The problem

Routine steps take hours and errors surface late.

What you get

What you get

  • One workflow mapped, built and tested on your past records
  • Approval steps and limits for anything touching money or customers
  • A log of what ran and what was held for a person
  • A runbook and handover

Limits

  • Nothing is paid or sent without a person
  • No payments initiated from your bank accounts by software
