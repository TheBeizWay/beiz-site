AI team setup. Two assistants your staff actually use.

A fixed-price setup for businesses of 10 to 50 people: two AI assistants built in the tools you already pay for, on two named tasks, with written rules and trained staff. Then we check how they're going after 30 days.

Why start with two

Most businesses either let staff use AI with no rules, or spend months planning and never start. Two assistants on two real tasks is enough to prove the value, small enough to control, and quick to undo if it isn't working.

How it runs

  1. Workshop: one session with you and the people who do the work. We list the repetitive tasks, what information each one touches, and pick two.
  2. Build: two assistants set up in Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or a business AI plan you already have or choose. No custom software. Anything that reaches a customer, moves money or decides something about a person waits for a person's approval.
  3. Rules and training: a one-page AI use policy covering what information can go in, what needs approval and who owns each assistant, plus a one-hour training session.
  4. Day 30 check: usage, errors and time per task, before and after. We fix what isn't working and tell you plainly whether to add more.

What it isn't

It isn't custom software, integrations that need a developer, or a licence sale: you buy any subscriptions directly. Support after day 30 is optional and priced separately.
