Services

Your AI workforce, set up properly, on a three-week rollout plan.

Start with one defined workflow. We set up its agent, access, approval steps and tests inside the tools you already use, plus limits, approvers and a decision log for the AI already inside your software, like Xero and Microsoft 365. Further agents, and monthly monitoring, are scoped and priced separately.

The agents

Six roles agents can take on

Data scientist agent

Cleans your exports, joins the records, builds the cash and margin models and flags outliers. Never changes a source record.

Financial controller agent

Reconciles bank to ledger, matches bills to orders and timesheets, and prepares the month-end checklist. Never posts a journal or releases a payment.

CFO desk agent

Drafts the weekly cash view, board pack numbers and variance notes. Never sends anything to your board, bank or investors.

Systems agent

Watches integrations, data feeds and who has access, and flags new AI tools staff start using. Never changes a permission or a setting.

Compliance agent

Prepares AML/CTF client checks and screening, sets monitoring dates and files the evidence. Never clears a possible match or accepts a high-risk client.

Risk officer agent

Keeps the controls register current, logs every exception and drafts the monthly risk report. Never signs off its own controls.

You don't need all six on day one. Most businesses start with the one or two that save the most hours, and add the rest once those are working.

Every agent

Six controls every agent gets

A named role and owner

A written job description: what the agent does, the tools it may use, and the person it reports to.

Limits it can't cross

Read-only access where possible, and a delegation limit. Changed bank details, new suppliers and anything over the limit always go to a person. AI usage is billed by volume, so each agent also gets a monthly spending cap, logged usage and an off switch you control.

Human checkpoints

Anything that moves money, reaches a customer or decides something about a person waits for a human yes.

A log of every decision

What the agent did, why, and who approved it, kept in your own accounts.

Tested before it goes live

We run the agent over a few months of your past records and check each result against what actually happened. It moves on only when it meets a pass mark agreed with you in writing, and it's re-tested before any change.

Monthly monitoring and review

We read the log, check for drift and errors, and adjust the limits. Rules like the Privacy Act's automated-decision disclosures are covered as they apply to you.

How it runs

A three-week rollout, then a day-30 review

Week 1: map and limit

We list the repetitive work, pick the agents worth having, and write each job description with you.

Week 2: test and shadow

First the agents run over your past records and we check their results against what actually happened. Then they run alongside your team without acting, and we compare their answers with yours.

Week 3: live within limits

The agents start work. Anything over a limit comes to a person.

Day 30: review

Errors, time saved and exceptions, measured. We fix what isn't working and tell you plainly whether to add more.

The part demos skip

Getting an agent to work in a demo is the easy part. Most of the setup is what keeps it working after week one: checking inputs before it acts, holding anything it isn't sure about, logging every decision, failing safely when a supplier sends a scanned PDF or a system is down, and a person reviewing the exceptions. Purav is the one on your calls and the one who builds those checks.

Do you need one?

When you don't need an agent

Use what you already have when

  • The work happens in one system, on a simple rule: the bank rules and bill approvals built into your accounting software.
  • One person needs help drafting, summarising or searching: the AI licence your team already pays for.
  • It happens a few times a month and a checklist would do.

An agent is worth setting up when

  • The work crosses systems: ledger, job or practice software, inbox and screening service.
  • Records have to be checked against each other before anyone acts.
  • Your limits and approvers have to be enforced every time, with a log someone can review later.

It isn't an audit or assurance engagement, custom software that needs a development team, or a licence sale. You buy any subscriptions directly, in your name. We don't do tax returns or BAS. If your own software can do it, we'll say so on the fit call and you won't pay for a build.
