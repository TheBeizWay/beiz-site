AI workforce setup

Your AI workforce, set up properly, on a three-week rollout plan.

We set up AI agents that do real finance and admin work inside the tools you already use: a data scientist agent, an accounting agent, a reviewer agent and a governance agent. Each one has a job description, limits and a person it reports to. We also set the limits, approvers and decision log for the AI already inside your software, like Xero and Microsoft 365. Then we keep them all in check every month.

The agents

  • Data scientist agent: forecasts cash for the next 13 weeks, spots margin drift by job, and flags what's unusual.
  • Accounting agent: reconciles transactions, matches bills to orders and deliveries, and drafts invoices and reminders.
  • Reviewer agent: traces figures back to the ledger and holds anything it can't trace for a person to check.
  • Governance agent: keeps the decision log, the register of AI in use and the monthly board or owner report.

You don't need all four on day one. Most businesses start with the one or two that save the most hours, and add the rest once those are working.

Five controls every agent gets

  1. A named role and owner. A written job description: what the agent does, the tools it may use, and the person it reports to.
  2. Limits it can't cross. Read-only access where possible, and a delegation limit. Changed bank details, new suppliers and anything over the limit always go to a person.
  3. Human checkpoints. Anything that moves money, reaches a customer or decides something about a person waits for a human yes.
  4. A log of every decision. What the agent did, why, and who approved it, kept in your own accounts.
  5. Monthly monitoring and review. We read the log, check for drift and errors, and adjust the limits. Rules like the Privacy Act's automated-decision disclosures are covered as they apply to you.

How it runs

  1. Week 1, map and limit: we list the repetitive work, pick the agents worth having, and write each job description with you.
  2. Week 2, shadow mode: the agents run alongside your team without acting. We compare their answers with yours.
  3. Week 3, live within limits: the agents start work. Anything over a limit comes to a person.
  4. Day 30, review: errors, time saved and exceptions, measured. We fix what isn't working and tell you plainly whether to add more.

What it isn't

It isn't an audit or assurance engagement, custom software that needs a development team, or a licence sale. You buy any subscriptions directly, in your name. We don't do tax returns or BAS.
