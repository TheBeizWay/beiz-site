Services

Automate the repeatable work before you send it offshore

For small Australian accounting and bookkeeping firms. We build bank-rec prep, statement checks, client information requests and workpaper tie-outs into your own systems, and list every difference for your reviewer. Your registered agent stays responsible for the work.

In brief
  • For small Australian accounting and bookkeeping practices weighing up offshore staff for bank recs, data entry and workpaper prep.
  • We build the repeatable part into your own Xero, MYOB or practice software first, with every difference listed for your reviewer.
  • Your registered agent stays responsible for lodgement and advice. Beiz does no tax, BAS or audit work.
The problem

Cheaper hours don't help if review time goes up

The saving shrinks at review

Work that comes back needing onshore review and rework uses partner and manager time, the most expensive hours in the firm.

The process lives in someone's head

If the steps aren't written down, the handover fails whoever picks it up, onshore or offshore.

Client data leaves your systems

Sending client files to another team, in another country, adds privacy steps and access risk your firm has to manage.

Errors surface at lodgement

A wrong coding decision found after lodgement costs far more than one held for a person at month-end.

What we automate first

Four jobs that follow rules every month

Bank reconciliation prep

Bank lines matched to the ledger on agreed rules, with every unmatched or doubtful line listed for your staff to clear.

Supplier statements and bills

Statements compared with the ledger: missing and duplicate bills, part-payments, netted credit notes and payments in transit.

Missing client information

One structured request per client, each item tracked until it's answered, instead of email chains before every close.

Workpaper tie-outs

Ledger balances tied to bank, loan, payroll and super clearing figures, with the differences shown line by line for the preparer.

Questions firms ask

Is this instead of offshore staff?

It can be, or it can come first. Automating the repeatable steps and writing the procedure down makes any later handover cleaner, whoever does the work.

Does Beiz do tax, BAS or audit work for our clients?

No. Beiz does no tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements, tax or BAS agent services, statutory audit or assurance. Your firm keeps that work and the client relationship.

Which software do you work with?

Xero and MYOB, plus exports from your practice and job systems. If a feature you already pay for solves the problem, we'll tell you on the first call.

What about privacy if a step uses an outside service?

We list every service a workflow uses and where it processes data before you approve the scope. If your firm is covered by the Privacy Act, sending personal information overseas brings obligations under Australian Privacy Principle 8; the OAIC guidance is linked on this page. General information, not legal advice.

What we don't do

Beiz doesn't do tax returns, BAS or GST lodgements, tax or BAS agent services, statutory audit or assurance, legal advice or financial product advice, and never releases payments without a person. Your tax agent keeps that work.
