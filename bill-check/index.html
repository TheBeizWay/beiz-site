Home/Try it/Bill check

Free · runs in your browser · nothing is sent

Check your bills for duplicates and red flags.

Drop in a bills export from Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks, or any spreadsheet saved as CSV. Eight checks run on this device in a few seconds. Your file isn't uploaded, stored or sent to us.

Six months of bills from a made-up building company, with a few problems planted. Download the sample file to see the format.

What it checks

  • Possible duplicate bills: same supplier, same amount, within 14 days
  • The same invoice number used twice by one supplier
  • A supplier's bank details changing
  • Bills that look split to stay under your approval limit
  • Bills just under your approval limit
  • A large first bill from a supplier first seen in your file
  • Payments made on a weekend
  • ABNs missing or failing the check-digit test

Getting the file

In your accounting software, open the bills or supplier invoices list (or a payables report), pick a date range and export it as CSV. Six or twelve months is a good start. If it only exports to Excel, open the file and save it as CSV. The check needs a supplier, an amount and a date; invoice numbers, payment dates, bank details and ABNs make it sharper.

Common questions

Is my bills file uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser on your own device. Your file isn't uploaded, stored or sent to Beiz.

Which accounting software does it work with?

Any bills or payables export saved as CSV, including from Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks. It needs a supplier, an amount and a date; invoice numbers, payment dates, bank details and ABNs make it sharper.

Is this an audit?

No. It's eight quick checks. A flag is a prompt for a person to look, and many flags will have an innocent explanation.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The paid two-week cash and data check goes further, from your full exports, for $1,500 fixed.

What it doesn't do

It's eight quick checks, not an audit. A flag is a prompt for a person to look, and plenty will have an innocent explanation. It can't see your bank, your emails or the bills you never entered, and it doesn't tell you whether anyone did anything wrong.

Want this every week?

These checks are a small slice of the two-week cash & data check, and of the financial controller agent, which runs them on every bill before it's paid and holds anything odd for you.
