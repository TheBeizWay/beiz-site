Limitations

This fixed synthetic sample is not a file-upload tool. It uses AUD, one hourly rate per project, an assumed consistent tax basis and matching periods. It does not model multiple rates, fixed-fee revenue recognition, contractual caps, GST, write-offs, payment status or time cut-offs. Differences require investigation and are not fraud findings or assurance opinions.

No third-party scripts, AI calls, analytics, browser storage or network requests are made by this demo. Navigating the site links loads other pages. No identifying client records are included.
