AI can prepare the work. Your people approve the decisions.

Every agent is set up the same way, before it touches anything real.

  1. Written down

    One job, its limits and the person it reports to, signed off by you.

  2. Locked down

    It reaches only what that job needs. It can't pay anyone or send anything.

  3. Tested

    It runs over your past records and goes live only at a pass mark you agree in writing.

  4. Watched

    Every action is logged. We review the log monthly and re-test before any change.
