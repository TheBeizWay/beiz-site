02How it works

AI can prepare the work. Your people approve the decisions.

  1. Written down

    One job, its limits and the person it reports to, signed off by you.

  2. Locked down

    It reaches only what that job needs, and it can't pay anyone or send anything.

  3. Tested

    It runs in shadow mode over your past records and goes live only at a pass mark you agree in writing.

  4. Watched

    Every action is logged in your own accounts. We review it monthly and re-test before any change.
