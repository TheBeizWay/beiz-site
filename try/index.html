Your numbers · Model it

Make the decision before the cash runs out

A 13-week cash model for a made-up trade business. Move the sliders and watch the low point move. Every number can be opened up.

Held steady in this demoOpening cash $50,000 · sales $48,000 a week: 60% paid within a week, 40% on account (30-day terms until now) · suppliers 40% of sales, paid after 30 days · overheads $6,000 a week · super 12% with each pay run · buffer $40,000

Weeks under buffer
Cash in week 13

How each week is calculatedClosing cash = opening cash + customer receipts − supplier payments − wages and super − overheads. 60% of each week's sales arrive the following week. Invoices already sent arrive on the old 30-day terms; new invoices on account arrive on the terms you set.
What this demo leaves outGST and BAS, tax, loans, one-off costs and seasonality. A real model is built from your ledger with assumptions we agree and write down.
