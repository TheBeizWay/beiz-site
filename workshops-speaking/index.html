Services

Workshops, coaching and speaking

Practical sessions on putting AI and data to work in finance and small business. Worked examples use made-up data, so your people can run more of it themselves.

Workshops

From manual task to tested AI workflow

For

Owners, operations and finance staff in small businesses and startups who repeat the same admin every week.

Format

Half day, or two 90-minute sessions. Online or in person. Small groups so everyone works on their own process.

Participants will

  • Choose one workflow worth automating, and one to leave alone
  • Map its steps, data and exceptions
  • Decide where a person must approve, and the limits that apply
  • Estimate running cost and time released with your own figures
  • Leave with a completed Automation Candidate Canvas

Sample agenda

  • Where AI helps in admin and finance work, and where it doesn't: a live walk-through on made-up data
  • Map one of your processes (exercise)
  • Exceptions, approval points and what gets logged (exercise)
  • Running costs and time released, with your own figures
  • Your canvas and next steps

Takeaways

  • Your completed Automation Candidate Canvas
  • A controls checklist: limits, approvals, logging, off switch
  • The slides

Priced after we understand your audience and format.

Request a workshop proposal

From spreadsheet reporting to decision-ready dashboards

For

Owners and finance leads who report from spreadsheets and want numbers they can act on.

Format

Half day, online or in person. Works best with your finance lead and one person who uses the reports.

Participants will

  • Agree the handful of measures that drive your decisions
  • Define each measure so it means the same thing every month
  • Spot the data-quality problems that make reports untrustworthy
  • See a worked dashboard built on made-up data
  • Set a simple refresh and review routine

Sample agenda

  • What decisions your reports should support
  • Measures and definitions (exercise)
  • Data sources and quality checks (exercise on a sample export)
  • Worked example: from export to dashboard, on made-up data
  • Your measure sheet and next steps

Takeaways

  • A measure-definition sheet for your business
  • A data-quality checklist
  • The worked example's structure to reuse

Coaching

Coaching for finance professionals

For

Accountants and finance leads building practical data and AI skills.

Format

A short series of one-to-one sessions, agreed in advance, each tied to something you're building.

You'll leave with

  • A working model, report or automation built by you, on made-up or approved data
  • Your build reviewed and explained, with the reasoning written down
  • A plan for the next skill to practise

Skills coaching only: not career, financial or legal advice. No employer or client confidential data in sessions.
