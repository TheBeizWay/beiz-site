Why trust Beiz

What we commit to, what we won't do, and how to start small.

Hiring someone to touch your finance data or AI is a trust decision. Here is the plain version: the promises we make, the work we refuse, and three ways to start where you risk very little.

Four commitments

Promises you can hold us to

01 · Price

Fixed scope, fixed price, in writing

You get a one-page proposal with the exact price and timeline within three business days. Nothing starts until you agree to it. Beiz is not registered for GST, so no GST is added.

02 · People

A person approves what matters

Anything that touches money, customers or decisions about people is approved by a human. Agents prepare and flag; they do not post, pay or decide.

03 · Independence

No software of our own to sell

Work is built inside your own accounts, such as Xero, MYOB, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. If you leave, you keep what was built.

04 · Data

AI plans that exclude training on your data

We use AI plans whose terms exclude training on client data. Please do not send passwords or bank details; exports are enough for most checks.

Clear limits

What Beiz does not do

Being clear about the edges is part of being trustworthy. If you need one of these, we will say so and point you to the right professional.

  • No tax returns or BAS, and no tax agent or BAS agent services.
  • No statutory audit or assurance.
  • No legal advice. Where a client needs legal wording, such as Privacy Act disclosure text, we draft it for their lawyer to review.
  • No financial product advice.
  • No claim that AI guidance is law. Australian AI guidance is voluntary; the Privacy Act automated-decision rules apply only to organisations the Act covers.

Questions people ask first

Straight answers

Will AI make decisions about my money?

No. Agents prepare, check and flag. A named person approves anything that touches money, customers or decisions about people, and each decision is logged so it can be reviewed later.

What data do you need from me?

For the cash & data check, exports from your accounting and timesheet tools, not logins. For the compliance sprint, staff interviews and a list of the AI tools in use; no ledger access.

What if it is not a fit?

We say so, in writing, and often point you to someone better placed. There is no chasing and no pressure.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Beiz does not provide tax agent, BAS agent, audit, legal or financial product advice. Where legal wording is needed, it is drafted for your lawyer to review.

What do I own at the end?

The register, models, policies and workflows are built in your own accounts and documented, so they stay with you.
