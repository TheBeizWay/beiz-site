Home/Cash & data check

Two-week cash & data check · $1,500 fixed

Find where your business is losing money.

We join your bills, invoices and timesheets and check them against each other. You get every finding with the record behind it, and what to do first.

Book the checkSee a sample report

What we check

  1. Bills against timesheets and quotes. Subcontractors billing more hours than were worked, or above the agreed rate.
  2. Hours worked against invoices. Work your team logged that never made it onto an invoice.
  3. Possible duplicate bills. The same bill entered twice, sometimes with a different number.
  4. Invoices against your rate card. Old rates or wrong item codes.
  5. Credits and overdue accounts. Supplier credits nobody applied, and customer invoices more than 30 days overdue.

Your accounting software checks what's on each bill. These checks compare it with what actually happened on the job.

What you get

  • A findings register: every item, the record it came from, the amount and what to do
  • A two-page report, written for the owner
  • A 30-minute walkthrough
  • What would stop each problem coming back, with a fixed-price quote if you want help

Common questions

What do you need from us?

Exports of your bills, invoices and timesheets or job records, for up to 12 months, plus your rate card and any quotes or purchase orders for the jobs we look at. Exports only: we don't need logins and nothing gets installed.

Which software does it work with?

Xero is the most common, alongside job or timesheet software. MYOB and QuickBooks exports work too. If your records are in spreadsheets, that's fine.

What does it cost?

$1,500, fixed and agreed in writing before we start. It's credited against the next piece of work if you go ahead. Beiz isn't registered for GST, so no GST is added.

How long does it take?

Two weeks from when we receive the exports, including a 30-minute walkthrough of what we found.

What isn't included?

Fixing the records, tax, BAS or GST work, and statutory audit. The check finds and explains; you decide what to do, and we can quote for the fixes.

Who sees our data?

Purav does the work himself. Your files are used only for the check, under a written engagement, and returned or deleted at the end.

Want to know what your records would show?

Send a few lines about your business and the software you use. You'll get a written reply by the next business day.
