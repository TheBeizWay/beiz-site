Services

Two-week cash & data check

Your bills, invoices and timesheets checked against each other, with every finding traced to its record.

In brief
  • For owners and finance leads who bill time or fixed fees and suspect work or money is slipping.
  • Two weeks, from your exports: bills, invoices and timesheets checked against each other, every finding traced to its record.
  • No logins to your ledger, no tax or BAS advice. The fee is credited if you go ahead with a build.
The problem

Bills, timesheets and invoices sit in different systems, so nobody can say which hours never reached an invoice or which bills don't match the job.

What you get

What you get

  • Bills, invoices and timesheets joined from your exports
  • A findings register: each item confirmed, needing information or explained, with the record behind it
  • A short read-out of what to look at first

Limits

  • Works from exports, with no logins to your ledger
  • Reports what the records support; some findings need your explanation before action
  • No tax or BAS advice
How it runs

Talk or write

A 20-minute call or a few lines in writing. A written reply by the next business day.

Fixed proposal

Within three business days: fee, timeline and definition of done.

Build and test

In your own tools, tested on your past records. Anything involving AI runs alongside your team first.

Hand over

Documentation, procedures and a review once it's running.

Fee

A fixed fee, agreed in writing before work starts

Timing

Two weeks

Credited once against your next Beiz build (cash model, AI workforce setup, compliance sprint or leave cover setup) if you accept the proposal and pay the deposit within 60 days of the findings. Not redeemable for cash; not for monthly support.
