Services

System-to-ledger exception review

One recurring month-end process mapped, sample inputs inspected, and a bounded fixed-price scope for fixing it in the tools you already use.

In brief
  • For owners and finance leads who bill time or fixed fees and suspect work or money is slipping.
  • Two weeks, from your exports: bills, invoices and timesheets checked against each other, every finding traced to its record.
  • No logins to your ledger, no tax or BAS advice. The fee is credited if you go ahead with a build.
The problem

The software is paid for, but the month-end still runs on exports, re-keying and a spreadsheet nobody wants to own.

What you get

What you get

  • The process mapped step by step, with the manual handoffs marked
  • Sample inputs checked: what's missing, what conflicts, what breaks
  • What your existing tools already do, and the gap they leave
  • A fixed-price sprint scope with written acceptance tests

Limits

  • One process per diagnostic
  • If a feature you already pay for solves it, we'll say so
  • No tax or BAS advice
How it runs

Talk or write

A 20-minute call or a few lines in writing. A written reply by the next business day.

Fixed proposal

Within three business days: fee, timeline and definition of done.

Build and test

In your own tools, tested on your past records. Anything involving AI runs alongside your team first.

Hand over

Documentation, procedures and a review once it's running.

Fee

A fixed fee, agreed in writing before work starts

Timing

About two weeks

Where the proposal says so, the fee is credited once against the exception sprint if you accept it and pay the deposit within 60 days. Not redeemable for cash; not for maintenance.
