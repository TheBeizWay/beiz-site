Interactive simulation

Chasing missing receipts, with a person in charge.

Rules find the gaps, a wait gives people time, AI drafts and matches, and a person approves every message and every match. Step through it below.

Interactive simulation · synthetic data · nothing is sent

Find transactions with no receipt attached.

A rule reads the attachment flag on each transaction for a made-up design studio. No AI is needed for this part: it's a yes or no.

Step 2 · Rules

Check exceptions before anyone is chased.

Your rules decide what never needs a receipt. A reminder log stops the same person being chased twice in a week. Anything the rules can't place is held for a person, not guessed.

Step 3 · Wait

Give people three business days to attach receipts themselves.

Many receipts turn up without a reminder. The wait is your setting; here it's simulated.

  • T5 Uber trip: receipt attached by Jo.Dropped from the reminder list.
  • T8 Zoom: a week since the last reminder.Now eligible again, so it joins Sam's reminder.
  • T7 Bunnings: still not linked to anyone.Stays with a person until someone says whose card it is.
Step 4 · AI drafts

One reminder per person, not one per transaction.

Grouping prevents a flood of messages. Jo's draft asks a question instead of assuming T6 is a second trip.

Step 5 · Person

Nothing is sent until a person approves it.

A person approves every message. In this simulation, approving only records the decision.
