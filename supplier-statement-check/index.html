What it checks
  • Bills and credit notes on the statement that aren't in Xero, and the reverse
  • Possible duplicate bills in Xero: same amount and date under a different reference
  • Amounts that differ between the statement and Xero
  • Credit notes the supplier has netted into an invoice
  • Part-payments and batched payments, matched payment to payment
  • Payments made after the statement date, shown as in transit
  • A line-by-line bridge from the statement balance to the Xero balance
Getting the files

From the supplier: their statement as CSV, or copy it into a spreadsheet with Date, Type, Reference and Amount columns. From Xero: export that supplier's bills, credit notes and payments for the same period as CSV. Amounts can be positive; the Type column (invoice or bill, credit, payment) sets the direction. Dates as DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.

Common questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Both files are read by your browser and checked on your device. Nothing is sent to Beiz or anyone else, and closing the page clears it.

What does it handle that a simple match doesn't?

Reference formats that differ (INV-00412 against 412), part-payments and batched payments, credit notes the supplier has netted into an invoice, possible duplicate bills, and payments made after the statement date.

Which Xero report should I export?

Any CSV of that supplier's bills, credit notes and payments with a date, reference, type and amount works. You'll see how each column was read before you rely on the result.

Is this a reconciliation sign-off?

No. It's a free first pass that lists what to look at. A person still checks each item before anything is changed or paid. General information only, not advice.

Limits

References are matched on their letters and numbers, so very different numbering schemes may not match. Payments are matched by amount within 10 days, or in groups of up to three. Opening balances aren't included, so use the same period in both files. It's a first pass that lists what to look at, not a reconciliation sign-off, and the figures in the sample are made up.
